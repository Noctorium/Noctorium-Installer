Every YouTube Music playlist shows up, however many you have, and the queue opens beside the page on the desktop —
and every Noctorium is version 0.23.0.

## Fixed

**All your YouTube Music playlists.** With more playlists than YouTube Music sends at once — a couple of dozen —
only the first lot showed up in the library, on the phone and on the desktop alike, and in Noctorium Rust. YouTube
sends the rest a page at a time, and only the first page was ever asked for. Every page is read now.

## New

**The queue beside the page.** On Noctorium for the desktop, the player bar's queue button opens the queue down the
right of the window, beside whatever page is open, as the lyrics button opens the lyrics: what plays now and what
comes after, to play from, reorder, shuffle, clear or save, with a button to the full Queue page. The lyrics and the
queue share the panel — each button switches it to its own, and closes it when pressed again — and the button for the
one open is lit. **Ctrl+U** (Cmd+U on a Mac) opens and closes the queue, as Ctrl+L does the lyrics.

## Worth knowing

- Every Noctorium — the desktop, the phone, the CLI, Noctorium Stats on both, Noctorium Rust, the web player and the
  installers — is numbered 0.23.0.
- The phone and the CLI are built on the same core as the desktop again, so they have its last fixes too: a Spotify
  import says what it made in the library before it says it is done.
- Noctorium Rust 0.21.1 and later installs this release by itself on Windows, unless that is switched off in
  Settings › Updates.
