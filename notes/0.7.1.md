Noctorium in any browser with nothing to install, and Noctorium from a terminal in one line.

## New

**The web player, hosted.** [noctorium-music.vercel.app](https://noctorium-music.vercel.app) — or
noctorium.vercel.app/music — is Noctorium in a browser, with nothing to install and no account to make.
YouTube Music and SoundCloud in one search, one queue and one player, now playing with synced lyrics, every
theme and seek bar, the accent taken from the cover, a sleep timer, a radio from any song, and similar songs
when the queue runs out.

- Likes and the playlists you make are kept in the browser, and can be saved as a file and opened in
  another. Playlists and albums you find can be saved to your library there too.
- SoundCloud plays straight from SoundCloud's servers. YouTube plays in YouTube's own player, which YouTube
  asks be shown: over the cover on the now playing screen, and as a small card in a corner otherwise.
- ListenBrainz works there as well, from the browser itself.
- Your own YouTube Music and SoundCloud accounts stay with the apps and with `noctorium web`, which serves
  the same page from your computer with everything signed in behind it.

**Install in one line.** In PowerShell on Windows, or a terminal on Linux:

```powershell
irm https://noctorium.vercel.app/install | iex
```

```bash
curl -fsSL https://noctorium.vercel.app/install | sh
```

Either fetches `noctorium-installer-cli` from the latest release, checks it against `SHA256SUMS.txt`, and
runs it — which asks whether you want Noctorium, the Noctorium CLI or both. Options go after it:
`… | sh -s -- --product cli` on Linux; on Windows, see the
[README](https://github.com/Noctorium/Noctorium-Installer#readme).

## Changed

- **The web player `noctorium web` serves** says Album over an album, and on a phone puts the best search
  match above the tracks rather than beside them, where it ran off the screen.
