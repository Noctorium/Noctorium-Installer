Two-step verification for your Noctorium account, and the account's own home at account.noctorium.xyz. Every Noctorium is version 0.39.0.

## New

- **Two-step verification.** Turn it on at [account.noctorium.xyz](https://account.noctorium.xyz), under *Security*:
  - an **authenticator app** (Google Authenticator, Aegis, 2FAS, 1Password…): scan the QR code, and keep the ten
    backup codes it gives you for a lost phone;
  - **Telegram codes**, which @NoctoriumLoginBot sends you;
  - or **both**: then pick either one at each sign-in, or turn on **strict mode** to need both.
- **Every sign-in asks** — email and password, Discord or Telegram — on the website and in every Noctorium: the
  desktop, the phone, the TV, the terminal (and `noctorium web`), Noctorium Rust and both Noctorium Stats. Noctorium
  Lite, the iPhone and the web player sign in through the website, which asks there.
- **account.noctorium.xyz and stats.noctorium.xyz.** The account — signing in, security, what it is linked to — has its
  own site; your listening figures are at stats.noctorium.xyz. Signing in on one counts on the other.

## Changed

- Everybody was signed out once when the account moved: sign in again, and link Last.fm and ListenBrainz again on the
  account page.
- Noctorium 0.38 and older still sign in, except with email and password to an account that has two-step verification
  on: update, or use *Sign in with Discord or Telegram*, which asks for the code in the browser.
