## If you use Last.fm: connect it once more

Noctorium's Last.fm application has a new name, and Last.fm ties every sign-in to the application that
made it. So after updating, Settings → Scrobbling says **Last.fm needs connecting again**. Press Connect,
approve Noctorium in your browser, and that is it. Anything you play before then is kept and sent to
Last.fm once you have. ListenBrainz is not affected.

## Desktop

**Keep playing in the tray.** Settings → **Startup and tray** → When you close Noctorium: **Quit**, as it
always has, or **Keep playing in the tray**. From the tray, click the icon to open the window again, or
right-click it for Play, Next, Previous and Quit. The first time it closes into the tray, it says so.

**Start with Windows.** On the same page: **Off**, **Open the window**, or **In the tray**, where it waits
out of the way until you want it. Switching it off in Task Manager's Startup tab works too, and Settings
shows that. On Linux this is "start when you sign in", for desktops that read the usual autostart folder.

**One Noctorium at a time.** Opening Noctorium while it is already running, in the tray or behind other
windows, brings that window forward instead of starting a second player beside it.

**Close Noctorium before installing an update over it**, and now that includes quitting it from the tray.

## Phone

**Change the lyrics source on the lyrics.** Every source sits in a row above the words, the ones that
found the song first. Tap one to switch. Noctorium then opens later songs on that source whenever it has
them, and on the best of the others when it does not. Settings → Lyrics shows the same choice, with **The
best answer** to go back. On the desktop, picking a source above the lyrics now sticks the same way.

**Settings, organised.** A short list in sections (your account, services, look and feel, listening, and
Noctorium itself), each line saying where it stands and opening a page of its own, the way the desktop's
Settings does. Back returns to the list.

**Lyrics no longer run into the song's title.** They fade out at the edges instead of being cut off.

## Both

**Three crimson themes:** Crimson, Scarlet and Garnet. Pure black pages for OLED screens, with red in the
panels and the accent. Under Customization → Theme.

**Spotify in one step.** Noctorium now brings its own Spotify app, so Connect Spotify is all there is to it,
with no client ID to make first. Using your own Spotify app is still there, folded away under **Use your
own Spotify app**, and if you already set one up it keeps working as it is.

## Worth knowing

- While Noctorium's Spotify app is new, Spotify only lets it read the accounts that have been added to it.
  If yours is not one yet, Noctorium says so after you sign in, and your own Spotify app is the way around
  it.
- "Start with Windows" needs an installed Noctorium. A copy run from its source cannot start with the
  computer, and says so.
