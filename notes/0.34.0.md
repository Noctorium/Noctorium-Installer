A first-time setup, a better pull-down search, and Song Find's notifications. Every Noctorium is version 0.34.0.

## New

**A first-time setup** on a first start of Noctorium — on the computer, the phone, Noctorium Rust, the web player and
in the terminal: a theme, where searches go, autoplay, and signing in, a step at a time. Skip it whenever you like;
everything in it is in Settings too. It is shown once: not after an update, not after a restart, and not at all if you
were using Noctorium before this version. Closed halfway, it is offered again until it is finished or skipped.

**Pulling down on the phone's Home** now shows two small menus above the search bar: which services to search —
Hybrid, YouTube Music, SoundCloud and the rest — and what to look for — Music, Radio, Artists or Playlists.

## Song Find

- **"Found it" or "No song found"** is now a notification whenever a search ends, not only when Song Find was off
  screen.
- **The countdown while listening** shows in the status bar, and a new notification counts the seconds while the song
  is looked up, saying who is being asked.
- **Notifications are asked for again** if they were turned down, and Settings says when Android has them off, with a
  button to turn them on.
