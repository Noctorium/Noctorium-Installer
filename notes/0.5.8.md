A fix for SoundCloud playlists: adding a track to one could leave it holding nothing but that track.

## Fixed

**Adding to a SoundCloud playlist no longer empties it.** SoundCloud saves a playlist's tracks all at once
rather than one at a time, so adding a track means reading what is already there and saving it back with
the new one on the end. When SoundCloud refused that read, or the connection dropped part way, Noctorium
took the playlist for empty and saved it with only the new track. Now it changes nothing and says so:
"SoundCloud would not say what is in that playlist, so nothing was changed." Try again in a moment.

Taking a track out of a SoundCloud playlist works the same way.

## Worth knowing

- Updating does not bring back tracks a playlist already lost this way.
- Nothing else changes in this release, on the desktop or the phone.
