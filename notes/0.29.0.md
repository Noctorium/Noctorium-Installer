A remote control for Noctorium on the desktop, in any browser tab or from a script, and every Noctorium is version
0.29.0.

## New

**Remote control, at localhost:6932.** Turn on *Settings › This device › Remote control* in Noctorium on the desktop
and open <http://localhost:6932> in a browser on the same computer. It is off until you turn it on.

- **Everything the player does.** Play and pause, next and previous, seek on the progress bar, the volume and mute,
  shuffle, repeat, speed, the sleep timer, stop after this song, autoplay, and liking a song — with the song's cover,
  and *LIVE* for a radio station.
- **The queue.** Click a song to play it, move songs up and down, remove them, shuffle or clear what is next, and
  keep, drop or play autoplay's suggestions.
- **Lyrics.** Synced lyrics follow the song, and clicking a line takes the song there. Pick another source for them,
  or have Noctorium look again.
- **Search.** Music or radio: play a result now, play it next or add it to the queue. Paste a link to play it or
  queue it. Your playlists and what you played recently are there too.
- **From the keyboard.** Space plays and pauses, the arrows seek and change the volume, and Shift with the arrows
  skips.
- **For scripts and stream decks.** Every button is an HTTP call: `curl -X POST localhost:6932/api/next`, and
  `localhost:6932/api` lists them all. The settings page shows a few to start from.
- **Another port.** If something else already uses 6932, Settings says so and lets you pick another.

## Worth knowing

- Only this computer can reach the remote control: it does not listen on your network, and other websites open in
  your browser cannot press its buttons.
- It is on the Windows, Mac and Linux desktop app for now, not yet on the phone or in Noctorium Rust.
- Every Noctorium — the desktop, the phone, the CLI, Noctorium Stats on both, Noctorium Rust, Noctorium Song Find on
  both, the web player, the website, the account service and the installers — is numbered 0.29.0.
