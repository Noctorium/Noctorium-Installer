Everything here is the volume control, a week older and better shaped.

## The phone was playing louder than it said

Noctorium opens at 72%, and on the phone nobody ever told the player that. ExoPlayer's own default is
full volume, so every session began at 100% with the slider drawn at 72% — and the first touch of the
slider was a drop rather than the small nudge it looked like.

Measured against the phone's own audio framework: untouched, 0.0 dB. Now, -2.9 dB, which is 0.72 to
the decimal place. The desktop was never exposed to this — mpv is handed the volume on the command
line of every track, so the number reaches it whether or not anybody has touched anything.

## Volume behind a button, like the desktop

It arrived in 0.4.7 as a row of its own under the transport buttons, which is the wrong shape for a
phone. It cost a line of a screen with less of it to spare, and it left a slider directly beneath the
play button for a thumb reaching for pause to catch.

So: one speaker icon at the right-hand end of the bottom row, under where the seek bar finishes, and a
panel that pops out of it with the percentage, the slider, and Mute and Boost. Closed, the icon still
says which of muted, quiet or loud it is, and lights while the boost is on.

The chips in the panel are taller than the desktop's, which are sized for a mouse.

## And on the desktop

The collapsed volume button lit up when the volume went above 100%. Since 0.4.7 there is no such
thing — the boost became a filter and the volume stops at unity — so that test was quietly never true
again and the button had stopped saying anything at all while a boost was running. It now lights for
the boost, the same as the phone's.

Nothing else has changed since 0.4.7.
