Your Noctorium account inside Noctorium, with a profile picture, and a settings button you can put where you like. Every Noctorium is version 0.40.0.

## New

- **Profile pictures.** Upload one, or use your Discord or Telegram picture. It shows on Home in every Noctorium, in
  both Noctorium Stats, on [account.noctorium.xyz](https://account.noctorium.xyz) and in the Telegram stats bot.
- **Your account inside the app.** Tap your picture on Home to sign in or out, change the picture, your name, email or
  password, or delete the account, without leaving Noctorium. Two-step verification asks for its code here too. On the
  desktop, the phone, the web player and `noctorium web`, and Noctorium Rust; Noctorium Lite and the iPhone have it
  too, and the terminal has it under *Settings → Noctorium account*.
- **The settings button on Home.** On the phone your picture is top-left and the gear top-right; on the desktop and the
  web the gear and then your picture sit top-right. Prefer it the old way? Set *Settings button* to *Toolbar* (search
  Settings for it) to put Settings back in the sidebar or bottom bar.
- **Hide Queue on the phone.** Queue can now be turned off in the bottom bar's tabs; it still opens from Now playing.

## Changed

- Changing your email or password signs every other device out; the one you changed it on stays signed in.
- Everyone starts with the settings button on Home after updating.
