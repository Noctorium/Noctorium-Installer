Noctorium Connect on and off from its own page, and a Lyrics tab beside the queue in the Playlist layout.

## New

**Noctorium Connect, on or off where you look for it.** A switch at the top of the Connect page, on the computer,
the phone, in the terminal and in the browser `noctorium web` serves, with a line saying what on and off mean. Off,
this device stops looking for your others and they cannot see it — nothing is sent or listened for on the network —
and the page says Connect is off instead of listing what to check. Switching it off while a device is the remote
for another, or is being driven by one, ends that remote control; the music plays on where it is. The switch in
Settings is called Noctorium Connect too, and searching Settings for "connect" or "turn off connect" finds it.

**A Lyrics tab in the Playlist layout.** Right after the queue's tab, so it is always in the same place: the song's
lyrics with their sources, the line being sung lit and kept in view, keeping time with a song playing on another
device too. On the computer, the phone, in the terminal and in the browser.
