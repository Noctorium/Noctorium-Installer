Noctorium Connect works.

## Fixed

**Noctorium Connect connects.** Devices on the same network could see each other and never get any further. The
phone refused every request it made to another device: Android lets an app send plain web requests only where it
says it will, and Noctorium had said only to itself. Connect's requests now go over a connection of their own, signed
with your account's key as before, and everything else Noctorium fetches stays https. Tested both ways between a
phone and a computer on one Wi-Fi: found in a second, the music handed over at the same second, and driven from the
other device.

**And everything after connecting:**

- **A remote control.** Hand the music to another device and the Connect window becomes its remote: the song, how far
  in, a seek bar, previous, play and pause, next, and the volume — on the computer and the phone. While the music is
  over there, the play, next and previous buttons here, the media keys and a headset drive that device, rather than
  starting the same queue again here.
- **Bring it back** brings back the whole queue, at the song and the second it had reached, not just the one song.
- **Starting a song here** stops it on the other device.
- **What went wrong is said.** Tapping a device kept the window open only for an instant, and a failure was never
  shown. Now it stays, and says why: a device that does not answer — "its firewall may be keeping Noctorium out on
  this network" — or one signed in to another account.
- On the desktop, Connect is also in Settings, for the player bar layouts that have no Connect button of their own.
- On the phone, music handed to it while Noctorium is in the background needs Android's permission to keep playing;
  the Connect card says so and offers Android's own Allow.
- A device list no longer stays greyed out after a handover that could not start, and "controlled by" no longer
  lingers after the other device has gone.

## Worth knowing

- Both devices need to be signed in to the same Noctorium account, on the same network (not a guest network that
  keeps devices apart).
- On Windows, when the firewall asks about Noctorium, allow it on the network you are on. Windows calls many home
  networks Public, and its question ticks only Private — tick Public too, or set the network to Private.
