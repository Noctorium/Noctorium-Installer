Your playlists in the desktop's sidebar, and a tidier Home on the phone. Every Noctorium is version 0.41.0.

## New

- **Playlists in the sidebar** (desktop). Turn on *Settings → Playlists in the sidebar* and your library's playlists
  sit under the sidebar's pages — your own first, then liked songs, then the rest — scrolling on their own, so a long
  library needs no taller window. A click opens one. Off unless you turn it on.
- **Pick what the sidebar keeps** (desktop). The *In the sidebar* chips, right above it in Settings, take any page
  out of the sidebar or put it back. Home and Settings always stay.

## Changed

- **The phone's Home header.** "What is on, and what you were listening to" now has a line of its own under your
  picture, the greeting and the buttons, instead of squeezing between them.
