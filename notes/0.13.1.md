Noctorium 98: Windows 98, at night.

## New

**Noctorium 98**, a theme beside 98 and XP in Settings › Theme. It is everything the 98 theme is — bevelled
buttons and boxes, title bars with their gradient, scroll bars with arrows, Settings as a Control Panel, Now
playing in windows on the desktop, the taskbar with its start button and clock — drawn in Noctorium's colours
instead of 98's grey:

- a dark violet face, lit along the top and the left of every button, so a button still stands up and a
  pressed one still sinks in;
- lists and fields as black as Night's panels;
- title bars from a deep violet into Noctorium's own, and Noctorium's violet for whatever is selected;
- and behind the windows, a night sky instead of the teal — with stars, on the desktop.

It is on every Noctorium: the desktop, the phone (whose status and navigation bars turn light to match), the
terminal, `noctorium web` and the player in the browser at [noctorium-music.vercel.app](https://noctorium-music.vercel.app).
Twenty themes now.

## Changed

- 98 is drawn from a scheme of its colours on every player, as 98 itself was, rather than from its grey fixed
  in place. Pick 98 and it is exactly as it was.
- On the phone and in the terminal, a theme's name is said once: "Noctorium 98", "Solarized Dark" and "Nord"
  rather than "Windows Noctorium 98", "Solarized Solarized Dark" and "Nord Nord".
