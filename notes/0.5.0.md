Noctorium can be made to look like a lot of different things now.

Everything here is appearance, and every setting is shared: pick it on the desktop and the phone
agrees, because both players read the same numbers.

## Liquid glass

Panels and cards go translucent, and — this is the half that is easy to forget — the page behind them
stops being flat and becomes a wash of the cover that is playing. Either alone is a disappointment.
Translucent panels over flat black are just slightly different panels, and a blurred cover behind
solid panels is a wallpaper nobody can see.

The wash mixes toward *the theme's own background* rather than toward black, so it stays quiet on
Night and equally quiet on Latte instead of leaving a light theme with a bruise on it. With nothing
playing there is no cover and no wash, which is the honest answer rather than a slab of glass over
nothing.

Solid is still the default, and not out of timidity: a music library is a wall of artwork, and the
panels between the covers are meant to be the quiet part.

## Five seek bars

**Minimal** and **Material** as before, and three new ones:

- **Wave** — the part that has played is a wave, and it travels while the music does. It settles flat
  when you pause rather than freezing mid-crest.
- **Segments** — the track in even blocks, filling one at a time.
- **Capsule** — one thick rounded bar, filled from the left. No handle, nothing else.

On the phone this setting used to do nothing on the now playing screen: that bar was a standard slider
whatever you had chosen, and the choice only reached the thin line in the player bar. It works there
now, which means the two styles that already existed are new to that screen as well.

## Corners, and text size

**Corners** — Sharp, Soft or Round, everywhere at once. Soft is exactly what Noctorium drew before.

**Text size** — Small, Default, Large or Largest. It scales everything together rather than a handful
of labels, and it multiplies whatever text size the machine or the phone is already set to, so if you
have set both you get both.

## Everything else

Unchanged. The player, accounts, downloads, scrobbling, Connect and both installers are as they were
in 0.4.9.
