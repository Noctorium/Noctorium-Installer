Your Spotify playlists in Noctorium without a working Spotify sign-in: public playlists followed by their links,
and everything else imported from Spotify's own data download.

## New

Spotify now answers Noctorium's sign-in only while the account that owns Noctorium's Spotify app has Premium, so for
most listeners the sign-in brings nothing. These two ways need no sign-in
at all. Both are under Settings → Spotify, on the computer, the phone, in the terminal and in the browser
`noctorium web` serves, and every song plays matched to the same recording on YouTube Music, as Spotify's always have.

**Public playlists, by their links.** In Spotify choose Share, then Copy link to playlist, and paste it under
Settings → Spotify. The playlist joins your library beside Spotify's own and is read again every time the library
is, so songs added or taken out on Spotify follow along. Paste a Spotify playlist or album link anywhere Noctorium
takes a link and it opens straight away; a playlist opened that way has **Keep in library** to follow it.

A warning above the box says what this cannot do: only public playlists, and only their first 100 songs, because
that is all Spotify's public page shows — a longer playlist is marked "first 100 songs". Private playlists and your
Liked Songs need the import below. The page is Spotify's to change, and if it does this stops working until
Noctorium is updated.

**Import from Spotify's data download.** On spotify.com, open your Account, then Privacy settings, and under
"Download your data" ask for Account data. Spotify emails a link to `my_spotify_data.zip`, usually within a few days.
Choose that file under Settings → Spotify, as it is — there is no need to unpack it. Every playlist you made becomes
a playlist of Noctorium's own, and your Liked Songs become "Liked Songs from Spotify".

It is a copy as it was when Spotify made the file: for songs added since, ask for a new file and import it, which
updates the playlists the last import made and never your own. Podcasts, audiobooks, files on your own device and
saved albums are left out, and as the file has no song lengths, songs are matched on their names and artists alone.

In the terminal: `noctorium spotify follow <link>`, `noctorium spotify unfollow <link>` and
`noctorium spotify import <file>`.

## Better

- Spotify's sign-in, for whoever it still works for, and playing on Spotify itself with Premium are just as they were.
  When Spotify refuses the sign-in, its message now says which two ways work without it.
- On the phone, Spotify's card no longer says any account works.
- In the terminal, Spotify's message wraps instead of being cut off, and a pasted link that cannot be opened says why.
- On the phone, a library row's second line takes two lines rather than being cut short.

## Worth knowing

- If your browser unpacks downloads by itself, as Safari on a Mac does, choose `Playlist1.json` and then
  `YourLibrary.json` from the folder: each import adds to what the other brought in.
- The browser player at noctorium-music.vercel.app does not have either of these; `noctorium web` does.
