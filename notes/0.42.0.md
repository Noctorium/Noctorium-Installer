A new player on the phone, a new song menu, glass your way, and Stats inside Noctorium. Every Noctorium is version 0.42.0.

## New

- **A new default player** (phone). The speed sits up top beside the sleep timer and the lyrics, the like, the menu and
  the volume spread out under the controls, and the queue is a swipe up from *Up next* at the foot of the screen. The
  player as it was is still there as *Old default*, in *Settings → Now playing → Layout*.
- **The song menu as a sheet** (phone). A song's ⋮ opens from the bottom: the song at the top with its like, Play next,
  Save to playlist and Copy link as big buttons, and the rest beneath. The old drop-down is *Settings → Now playing →
  Song menu → Old menu*.
- **Stats inside Noctorium** (phone). Tap your picture on Home for *Account* or *Stats*: your listens, time listened,
  top songs and artists, and the split by service, over 7 days, 30 days, a year or all time. Counted from the history
  on your phone, so it needs no account and sends nothing anywhere.

## Changed

- **Button and background surfaces apart** (phone). *Settings → Colour and surfaces* has *Button surfaces* and
  *Background surfaces*, each solid or liquid glass, so glass buttons can float over a plain page or solid ones over the
  cover's wash. If you had liquid glass, you keep it for both.
- **Glass search boxes** (phone). With glass buttons, the search box on Search and Home's pull-down search are glass too.
- **The phone's Home top bar** shows your name, smaller, with "What is on, and what you were listening to" under it.
