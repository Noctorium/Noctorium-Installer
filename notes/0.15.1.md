The volume booster on the computer works when you switch it on mid-song.

## Fixed

**The booster gets louder the moment you switch it on, on the computer.** While the booster is on, your volume
becomes part of the boost and the player itself plays at full volume. Switched on in the middle of a song, the player
never got that last step, so your volume was applied twice: at the volume Noctorium opens at, 200% came out quieter
than no boost at all, and higher levels gained far less than they should. Switching the booster off mid-song had the
opposite effect: the song jumped to full volume until the next one. Both now take effect a fifth of a second after
you move the booster, in the song that is playing.

It only happened on the song playing when the booster changed — the next song always started at the right level —
which is why it seemed to work some of the time.
