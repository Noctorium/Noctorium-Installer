A new yt-dlp, and nothing else: Noctorium 0.40.1 is 0.40.0 with [yt-dlp 2026.11.23](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp/releases/tag/2026.11.23).

## Worth knowing

- yt-dlp is what Noctorium on the computer reads YouTube, YouTube Music and SoundCloud with. They change their
  pages often, and a newer yt-dlp is what keeps them playing.
- This release was made by itself, the day yt-dlp released: every Noctorium is the same as in 0.40.0, numbered
  0.40.1.
