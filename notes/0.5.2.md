Paste a link and it plays, a Downloads tab, no more FFmpeg, and songs that stop on their own come back.

## New

**Paste a link.** A song or a playlist from YouTube Music, YouTube or SoundCloud: paste it and it plays.
On the desktop it is **Paste link** in the sidebar, and **Ctrl+V anywhere** in Noctorium plays the link on
your clipboard. On the phone it is the **Link** tab, or tap **Share** in the YouTube, YouTube Music or
SoundCloud app and pick **Play in Noctorium**. Links pasted inside a message, links without `https://`,
SoundCloud short links and links full of tracking all work. A link typed by hand plays when you press
Enter, so it cannot start the wrong song halfway through the typing.

**A Downloads tab** on both. Everything you have downloaded, what is still downloading and how far along
it is, and a box to download a song or a whole playlist from its link.

**No more FFmpeg.** Saving as MP3 no longer needs it, and Noctorium no longer offers to download it. mpv,
which is already there, makes the MP3, and Noctorium writes the title, artist, album and cover into the
file itself. Save as MP3 had actually been failing on computers without FFmpeg, with "Saving as MP3 needs
ffmpeg", which is why it looked as though you needed it. Saved MP3s now carry their cover too.

## Fixed

**"youtube-dl failed: unexpected error occurred", and songs stopping by themselves.** The message was a
side effect, not the cause: when a stream dropped partway, or YouTube stopped accepting its address, the
player fell back on a helper that could not deal with it, and stopped the song. On the desktop the song
now picks up again on its own, a second and a half before where it stopped, from a fresh address. On the
phone a dropped connection — walking from Wi-Fi onto mobile data — waits for the network to come back
and carries on, where before it went straight to an error.

**Blurry covers.** YouTube Music's listings give their covers at 120 pixels, and that was being stretched
across the whole Now Playing screen. Covers are now fetched at the size they are shown.

**Replaying a song is faster.** A song's stream address was forgotten after half an hour, though
YouTube's last for about six, so any replay after that waited for a fresh lookup. They are now kept for as
long as they last. The phone forgets them as soon as it changes networks, since they only work on the
network that asked for them.

**Also:**
- The desktop keeps its covers to a fixed amount of memory. Sharp covers are larger, and a long session
  could otherwise hold hundreds of megabytes of them.
- Error messages and logs no longer include the full stream address, which contained your IP address.
- On the phone, YouTube's automatic artist channels no longer show as the artist: "Burial - Topic" is
  "Burial", as it already was on the desktop.
- The desktop sidebar is a little wider, so "Now playing" and the new entries fit on one line.

## Worth knowing

- **The number keys on the desktop follow the new sidebar:** 1 Home, 2 Search, 3 Paste link, 4 Library,
  5 Downloads, 6 Now playing, 7 Queue, 8 Settings.
- **On the phone, with seven tabs, only the selected one shows its name**, so every name that shows is
  whole.
- If Noctorium downloaded FFmpeg for you before, it is left where it was and no longer used. You can
  delete `ffmpeg.exe` from `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Noctorium\bin`.
- On the phone, saved music is still the original m4a or opus, which Android plays as it is.
- On the desktop, a SoundCloud song opened from a pasted link is kept apart from the same song found
  through search, so a like or a download of one does not show on the other. YouTube links do not have
  this problem.
