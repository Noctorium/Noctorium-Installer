Artists' pages, finished. Every Noctorium is version 0.32.1.

## Better

**An artist now has a page of their own** rather than opening as a playlist, on the computer, the phone, Noctorium Rust
and the web player:

- Their picture — their own on SoundCloud, or four of their covers where the service shows none — with "Artist" and
  their name.
- The service, how many songs, and on SoundCloud how many follow them and their bio.
- Play, Shuffle, and "Open on …" their page on the service, then their songs, numbered.
- Back goes back to where you pressed their name — Search, Now playing — instead of into your library.

**On the phone,** tapping the artist on Now playing opens their page in front, rather than behind the Now playing
screen where it could not be seen. Back brings Now playing back.

**A song by several artists** — "ODESZA, Theodore Shapiro" — has a "Go to …" in its menu for each of them.

**Artists in Search** keep the service under each name, so the same artist on two services can be told apart, and
the web player only lists the artists whose name is what you searched for.

**In a narrow window** on the computer, an artist's page puts the picture above the name instead of squeezing it.

## Fixed

- In Noctorium Rust, "Open on the service" on an artist's page opened the wrong address.
- The web player at noctorium-music.vercel.app is up to date again, with everything from 0.32.0 — artists, Artists in
  Search, SoundCloud played from YouTube Music when it refuses, and links played from Search.
