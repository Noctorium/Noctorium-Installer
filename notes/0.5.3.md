More player bar layouts, on the desktop and on the phone.

## Desktop

Settings → **Player bar layout** now has six. Inline and Stacked are as they were, and there are four new
ones:

- **Centred.** The song on the left, the controls in the middle with the seek bar under them, and your
  tools on the right. The controls stay in the true centre of the window whatever the title's length.
- **Slim.** One short row, with the progress as a thin line along the edge that faces the page. The line
  is still a seek bar: click to jump, drag to scrub. Nothing is missing to make it short: shuffle,
  previous, play, next, repeat, like, download and all the tools are there.
- **Slim left.** The same row with the controls first and the song after them.
- **Spotlight.** A taller bar with a large cover and title, tinted with the colours of the artwork.

## Phone

The phone has a layout setting of its own now, under Settings → Customization → **Player bar layout**,
separate from the desktop's.

- **Classic.** The bar as it was.
- **Slim.** Shorter, with the song in two small lines and all five controls: shuffle, previous, play, next
  and repeat.
- **Slim left.** Slim mirrored: the controls at the left, where a left thumb reaches, and the cover at the
  far end.
- **Controls.** Previous, play and next beside the song, and a seek bar underneath that you can drag.
- **Spotlight.** A bigger cover and a big play button, over a blur of the artwork.

On every layout, tapping the bar still opens the song and swiping it still moves through the queue. All of
them work with Liquid glass and with a solid bar, and with the bar at the top or the bottom.

## Fixed

- **A settings value Noctorium did not recognise reset every setting.** If the settings file held a choice
  the running version had no name for, for example after going back to an older version, the whole file
  was treated as unreadable and everything went back to its default. Now only that one choice goes back to
  its default, and everything else is kept.
- On the desktop, rows of choices in Settings wrap onto a second line instead of running off the edge of a
  narrow window.

Nothing else has changed since 0.5.2.
