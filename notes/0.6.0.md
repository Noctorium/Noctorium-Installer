The now playing screen, arranged your way.

## New

**Six layouts for now playing**, on the desktop. Pick one under Customization → Now playing, where each
is shown as a small picture of itself:

- **Side by side:** the track, the controls and the cover on the left, the queue and lyrics beside them.
  The screen as it was.
- **Panel left:** the same, turned round.
- **Centre stage:** a large cover in the middle of the screen with the track and controls beside it, and
  nothing else.
- **Focus:** the cover, the track and the controls stacked down the middle.
- **Banner:** the track in one band across the top, and the queue and lyrics the full width beneath it.
- **Sing along:** the lyrics large and centred across most of the screen, with the cover and controls in
  a column beside them.

**Change it from the screen itself.** The sliders button under the cover opens a menu with the layouts,
the cover, its size and the backdrop. It stays open while the screen rearranges behind it, so you can
see each choice before making the next. The sidebar button beside it tucks the panel away, and the
record takes the middle of the screen until you bring it back.

**Four covers.** Rounded, Square, Circle, and **Record**: the cover set into a vinyl disc that slowly
turns while the music plays and stops where it is when you pause. Each in three sizes.

**Three backdrops.** The colour wash from before, the **blurred cover** behind everything, or Plain.

**And the rest of it:** how wide the panel is, whether it opens on Up next or Lyrics, and whether the line
being sung and the Follow button are shown.

## Worth knowing

- The **Ambient backdrop** switch under Colour has become the **Backdrop** choice under Now playing.
  Plain is the switch turned off, and whatever you had is kept.
- If **Animations** is off, the record does not turn and the layouts change without easing.
- In a narrow window the layouts with a panel stack it under the track, as the screen always did.
- The phone's now playing screen is unchanged.
