Noctorium comes to the car and the TV: Android Auto and Android TV, in the one Android app, and every Noctorium is
version 0.31.0.

## New

**Noctorium for Android Auto.** Plug your phone into the car, or connect without a cable, and Noctorium is in Android
Auto's music apps.

- **Home:** your Home's rows — what you pinned, *Jump back in*, and the services' own rows.
- **Library:** *Recently played*, your Noctorium playlists, and your playlists on the services you are signed in to.
- **Queue:** the song playing now, and what autoplay has lined up after it. Pick one to jump there.
- **Radio:** the most listened-to stations where you are.
- **Search and voice:** the car's search box, and *"play Daft Punk on Noctorium"*.
- **Covers** for every song.

The car and the phone are one player: what you pick in one shows in the other.

**Noctorium for Android TV and Google TV.** Install Noctorium on a TV and it is on the TV's home screen, with pages made
for the remote:

- **Now playing:** the song large, with its cover, where it is, and its buttons.
- **Home, Search, Library, Queue and Radio,** as on the phone.
- **Settings,** which opens Noctorium's full screens for signing in to the services.

A phone or computer running Noctorium on the same network finds the TV under Connect, and can play on it.

**The Android installer works on a TV too,** and lists itself on the TV's home screen.

## Worth knowing

- It is all one APK, `Noctorium-0.31.0.apk`, for phones, cars and TVs. Obtainium keeps it up to date the same way.
- Android Auto only lists apps from Google Play until it is told otherwise. To see Noctorium in the car, open Android
  Auto's settings on the phone, tap the version number ten times to turn on developer settings, and there turn on
  *Unknown sources*.
- The car's and the TV's screens come from two new repositories, Noctorium-Android-Auto and Noctorium-Android-TV,
  which Noctorium-Mobile builds in.
- Every Noctorium — the desktop, the phone with its car and TV, the CLI, Noctorium Stats on both, Noctorium Rust,
  Noctorium Song Find on both, the web player, the website, the account service and the installers — is numbered
  0.31.0.
