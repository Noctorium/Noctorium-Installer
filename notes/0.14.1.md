## Changed

- **Previous starts the song again** once it has played for five seconds or more; sooner than that, it goes to the
  song before. Pressed twice quickly it still steps back through the queue: the first press goes back to the start,
  the second to the song before. The first song in the queue, with nothing before it, goes back to its start. The
  same everywhere Previous is — the player bar, the keyboard, the phone's lock screen and notification, a terminal,
  the browser, and a device driving another with Connect.
- **A song picked from a search starts autoplay from it**, as one picked on Home does: the song alone, with songs
  like it after it, instead of every other result queued behind it. On every Noctorium, Ctrl+F's quick search and
  `noctorium play` in a terminal included. The choice is now *Playing a song from Home or a search*, in Settings ›
  Playback and queue, and *The songs around it* brings the rest of the results back.
