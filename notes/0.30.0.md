Noctorium Rust starts its move to Slint, and every Noctorium is version 0.30.0.

## New

**A new window for Noctorium Rust, drawn by [Slint](https://slint.dev).** Start Noctorium Rust with `--ui slint`
(or set `NOCTORIUM_UI=slint`) to try it. It is the same player underneath — your queue, library, accounts and
settings are shared with the usual window, and you can switch between the two whenever you like.

- **What it has so far:** the side menu with your playlists, the player bar, Home, Search (music and radio),
  Library, Liked songs, Recently played, your playlists and followed ones, the queue with autoplay, and Now playing
  with Up next and synced lyrics that follow the song — click a line to jump to it.
- **Every theme,** colour for colour, switched from its Settings page.
- **A menu on every song** (right-click, or ⋯): play next, add to queue, start a radio, like it, and in the queue
  move it or take it out.
- **The keyboard:** Space plays and pauses, ← → seek, Ctrl+← → skip, Ctrl+F searches, Ctrl+L opens the lyrics and
  M mutes.

## Worth knowing

- The usual window is still Noctorium Rust's own, and the one it opens in. Services, Connect, Downloads, Profile
  and most of Settings are only there for now; they move to the Slint window over the next releases, and once it has
  everything it becomes the only one.
- Building Noctorium Rust yourself now needs Rust 1.92 or newer, and on Linux fontconfig's development files.
- Every Noctorium — the desktop, the phone, the CLI, Noctorium Stats on both, Noctorium Rust, Noctorium Song Find on
  both, the web player, the website, the account service and the installers — is numbered 0.30.0.
