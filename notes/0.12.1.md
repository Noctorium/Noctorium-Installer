The queue a button away on the phone's Now playing, autoplay's own switch in the queue, and a song that will not play after the phone has slept starting again by itself.

## New

- **The queue from Now playing**, on the phone: a button beside the lyrics at the top opens the queue over the
  song, at the song playing. The arrow or back returns to the song. It can be put away in Customization ›
  Player buttons, like the others.
- **Autoplay's switch in the queue.** On or off right where its songs are: a switch on autoplay's heading on
  the phone, a chip beside Shuffle and Repeat on the desktop's Queue page, and a pill beside Shuffle and Clear
  in Now playing's Up next. Before, the queue could only turn autoplay on, and only while it was off.

## Fixed

- After hours with the phone locked, a paused song could refuse to play until another song was played and
  this one chosen again. Now:
  - A song told to play that has not moved for fifteen seconds -- loading for ever, or holding nothing --
    starts again where it was, from a fresh stream, twice before saying so.
  - Every press of play, in the app, on the lock screen or on headphones, gets its own tries to recover the
    stream; an hour-old dropped connection no longer leaves the song with none.
  - A song whose stream failed is paused as well, so the next press of play fetches a fresh stream instead
    of trying the refused one again.
  - A stream recovered while the song was paused stays paused, rather than starting by itself.
- A song that failed part-way, or was given up on while it loaded, picks up where it was when it is played
  again, rather than from the start. On every Noctorium.
- Playlists are saved one change at a time: one made and deleted a moment later no longer comes back after a
  restart, and a change made just before quitting is kept.
