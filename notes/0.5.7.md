Music that keeps playing with the phone locked, private SoundCloud playlists on the phone, playlists you
can change from it, and animations on both.

## If the music stopped when you locked your phone

Some phones close background apps when the screen locks, whatever they are doing, and music goes with
them. Xiaomi's do this by default. Noctorium now asks, the first time you play something, whether it may
keep running in the background: that is Android's own dialog, and you answer it.

On a Xiaomi, Redmi or POCO phone there is one more step. Settings → On this phone → **Keep playing when the
phone locks** → **Battery saver** opens that phone's own battery page for Noctorium. Choose **No
restrictions** there. The same card has **Allow**, for anybody who said "Not now" the first time.

## Phone

**Private SoundCloud playlists open.** A private playlist's address carries a secret on the end, and the
phone refused it with "URL not accepted". It is now read straight from SoundCloud, and a private track
inside it plays.

**Change a playlist from the phone.** On a YouTube Music or SoundCloud playlist of yours: **Private** or
**Public**, the one it is now lit, **Rename**, **Delete**, and a button to download the whole playlist.
The library marks the private ones.

**Playlists made in Noctorium open.** Tapping one did nothing on the phone. Now it opens, to play, put in
order with the arrows, take tracks out of, rename, delete, or copy up to YouTube Music or SoundCloud from
the ⋮ menu.

## Both

**Animations.** Screens ease in, pages and playlists slide in from the right and back out, a new song's
name rises into the player bar as the last one lifts away, play and pause swap with a small scale, a like
pops, rows glide into place when you reorder or remove one, the line being sung brightens into place, and
on the desktop a cover lifts a little under the pointer. If you would rather nothing moved, there is an
**Animations** switch under Customization; off makes every change instant.

**Public and private shown straight away.** Making a playlist public or private, or renaming it, now shows
the moment the service accepts it, rather than after the library has been read again.

## Worth knowing

- The playing-in-the-background choice is yours and lives in the phone's settings, not Noctorium's:
  Noctorium only opens the right page.
- Private tracks inside private SoundCloud playlists are new ground; if one will not play, the message
  says so.
