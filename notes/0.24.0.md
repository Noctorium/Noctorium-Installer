Noctorium playlists, on every device you use Noctorium on, and lyrics for songs uploaded under somebody else's name —
and every Noctorium is version 0.24.0.

## New

**Noctorium playlists.** A playlist can now be a **Noctorium playlist**: the same playlist on your computer, your
phone and Noctorium Rust, kept in storage of your own rather than on a server. Making a playlist asks which you want —
a Noctorium playlist, or a **local playlist** that stays on that one device — and a playlist's menu turns one into the
other. Noctorium playlists carry a cloud in the library.

Choose where they are kept in **Settings › You › Noctorium playlists**:

- **A synced folder** — on a computer, a folder that Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox or Syncthing already keeps in
  step (Noctorium offers the ones it finds in one click); on a phone, a folder its own file picker chooses.
- **Google Drive**, signed in to. Noctorium can see only the files it makes there, nothing else in your Drive. It
  needs a Google OAuth client of your own, made once in Google Cloud Console ("Desktop app" type, with the Drive API
  on); paste its id and secret on each device. On a phone, this is the way to Google Drive: its app does not lend
  whole folders to other apps.

Each playlist is one small file in a "Noctorium Playlists" folder there. They sync when Noctorium opens, a moment
after you change one, and every few minutes; the copy changed last wins, and a playlist deleted on one device is
deleted on the others.

**Lyrics for songs uploaded under somebody else's name.** A song uploaded by a channel — "Artist - Song (slowed +
reverb) [lyrics]" — was asked for under that title and that channel, and no lyrics source knew it. When none does now,
Noctorium looks again for the song under what is left of its title, and shows the closest that has lyrics, with a
small note above them that they may not be this song's. Close the note with its ✕, or switch it off for good in
Settings › Lyrics; the lyrics are found either way.

## Fixed

- On the phone, a playlist could only be made on YouTube Music or SoundCloud, and the library's New playlist button
  was missing with no account signed in. Both are there now, with Noctorium and local playlists.

## Worth knowing

- Every Noctorium — the desktop, the phone, the CLI, Noctorium Stats on both, Noctorium Rust, the web player and the
  installers — is numbered 0.24.0.
- Playlists you already have stay local until you make them Noctorium playlists.
