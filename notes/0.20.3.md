The song's length and how far in it is, in OnePlus's Live Alerts bubble, from the moment a song starts.

## Fixed

- **--:-- and an empty bar in OnePlus's Live Alerts bubble while a song played.** Noctorium told Android a song's
  length only once the stream had begun arriving, a moment after the song itself, and the bubble — which seems to
  read the song once, as it starts — was left with no length to show. Noctorium now gives the song's length the moment
  the song starts, so the bubble, the lock screen and the notification all have it from the first second. Once the
  stream is in, its own exact length takes over, as before.

## Worth knowing

- This was tried on a Xiaomi, where the song's length now arrives with the song rather than about a second after it;
  the OnePlus bubble itself was not to hand. If yours still shows --:--, say so in an issue.
