A new theme you can see through -- and every Noctorium is version 0.26.0.

## New

**Glass.** A theme in Settings › Look › Theme, with the others -- no code needed. The whole window turns to smoked
glass and your desktop shows through it, like the glass browsers. On Windows 11 the desktop is blurred behind the
window, and the window keeps its own title bar, so dragging, snapping and resizing work as they always have. The
pages, panels and cards are tinted just enough to keep everything readable, whatever your wallpaper is.

## Worth knowing

- **Windows 10 and 11:** see-through, with the system's blur (Acrylic on Windows 11).
- **Linux:** see-through as well. The window draws its own title bar there, since Linux only lets a window with no
  system frame be see-through. On KDE the desktop behind it is blurred; on GNOME and others it shows clear, and the
  pages are drawn a little more solid to make up for it. A desktop without a compositor gets the colours only.
- **macOS and the phone:** Glass is a cool dark slate theme there, without the see-through window for now.
  Noctorium Rust doesn't have Glass yet.
- Switching into or out of Glass rebuilds the window in the same place and size. The music keeps playing.
- Every Noctorium -- the desktop, the phone, the CLI, Noctorium Stats on both, Noctorium Rust, the web player and the
  installers -- is numbered 0.26.0.
