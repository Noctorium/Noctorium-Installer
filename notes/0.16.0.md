Each service's own logo on its badges, YouTube Music playlists you can make public, unlisted or private, and a
history of what you play that stays on your device.

## New

**Logos instead of letters.** The little badge that says where a song comes from — "YTM", "SC", "SP" — now shows
the service's own logo: YouTube Music, YouTube, SoundCloud, Spotify, Bandcamp and VK, and a folder for music on your
device. On the computer, the phone and in the browser, at the same size as before and in every skin. Prefer the
letters? **Service badge style → Letters**, next to the icon sets, puts them back; search Settings for "logo" or
"badge" to find it. The terminal player keeps its letters, since a terminal can only draw text.

**Your YouTube Music playlists, edited from the computer.** Your own playlists now say whether they are **Public**,
**Unlisted** or **Private**, and clicking that badge changes it. A new **Edit** button changes the name, the
description and who can see it, in one save. Playlists you only saved from someone else no longer offer Rename or
Delete once you open them.

**A history of what you play, kept on this device.** Every song you listen to, newest first and grouped by day, on
your profile — the **Noctorium Listener** tile in Settings, or whatever you named it — on the computer, and a new
**Your profile** page on the phone. Play one again, add it to the queue, filter by title, artist or album, remove one, or clear it all.

- A song goes in once you have heard half a minute of it (half of it, for one shorter than a minute), so a skip is not
  a listen.
- It stays on the device it was played on: it is not sent to your Noctorium account, your other devices, Connect or
  the stats, and on Android it is left out of Google Drive backups and moving to a new phone.
- While you control another device over Connect, that device keeps the history, not this one.
- **Keep a history of what I play** turns it off.

## Fixed

- **"Make public" and "Make private" were never offered for YouTube Music playlists.** Noctorium did not read a
  playlist's privacy from YouTube Music, and the button only appears once it is known. It is read now, whenever the
  playlist opens.

## Worth knowing

- On the phone, your name has moved from your account's page to the new **Your profile** page.
