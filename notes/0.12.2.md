The speed on a button of its own.

## New

- **A speed button on the desktop's player bar**, beside the volume, where it used to be the lower half of
  the volume's popover. A dial at normal speed, and the speed itself -- "1.25×", lit -- whenever it is not
  normal, so a song is never quietly playing fast. It opens the same slider, with its way back to normal, on
  every bar layout that has the volume, the Taskbar's tray included.
- **It can be put away** under Settings › Buttons on the player bar, like the others, and stays while the
  speed is not normal. A narrow window lets it go with shuffle, repeat and the sleep timer.
- On the phone, the speed button on Now playing can be put away in Customization the same way.
