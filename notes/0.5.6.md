Two themes for anybody who remembers the start-up sound, and a seek bar to match.

## New

**Windows 98 and Windows XP themes.** Under Customization → Theme, in a new Windows group, on the desktop
and the phone.

- **98:** the grey of its windows, white where a list sat, black writing and the navy of an old title bar
  for the accent.
- **XP:** XP's beige window colour, the pale blue of Explorer's side panel, and the blue of its taskbar
  for the accent.

Both are light themes. For the whole look, also set **Corners** to **Sharp** and the seek bar to
**Classic**; picking a theme leaves your other choices as they are.

**A Classic seek bar.** The sixth seek bar style, under Customization → Seek bar style: the old Windows
progress bar, a sunken strip that fills with square blocks in your accent colour, with a raised grey
handle to drag. It works with every theme. On the phone, the thin line under the player bar shows blocks
for it too.

## Worth knowing

- There are six seek bar styles now: Minimal, Material, Wave, Segments, Capsule and Classic. The list
  under Customization is longer than one screen, so the last ones are further down.
