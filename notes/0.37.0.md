Last.fm and ListenBrainz, kept with your Noctorium account: link them once, and every Noctorium you sign in to scrobbles there. Every Noctorium is version 0.37.0.

## New

- **Link Last.fm and ListenBrainz to your Noctorium account.** On service.noctorium.xyz, under *Scrobbling*: connect
  Last.fm on its own page, or paste your ListenBrainz token. Or connect them in any Noctorium while signed in, and they
  are saved to your account by themselves.
- **Signing in connects them.** The desktop, the phone, the TV, the terminal and Noctorium Rust connect the Last.fm and
  ListenBrainz linked to your account as soon as you sign in to Noctorium -- nothing to set up on each device.
  Disconnecting on one device leaves the others alone.
- **Noctorium Lite, the iPhone and the web player scrobble too**, through your account: what you play shows as
  "Scrobbling now", and every song you listen to through is scrobbled, by Last.fm's own rule.
- Your Last.fm session and ListenBrainz token are kept encrypted, and sent only to Last.fm, ListenBrainz and your own
  signed-in Noctorium apps.

## Changed

- Signing in to the Noctorium account is with Discord, Telegram or your email: the GitHub sign-in is gone.
