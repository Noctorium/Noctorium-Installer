The Noctorium account comes to Noctorium Lite and Noctorium for iPhone and iPad, and signing in with Telegram happens in the Telegram app. Every Noctorium is version 0.36.1.

## New

- **Noctorium Lite and Noctorium for iPhone and iPad have the Noctorium account.** Under *Library*, press *Sign in*:
  the account's page opens with a code, you sign in with Discord, Telegram or your email and press Allow, and the app
  signs itself in. Your listening is counted, and what you play shows as your Discord status and in your Telegram bio.
  On iPhone the sign-in is kept in the Keychain.
- **The web player has it too**, at music.noctorium.xyz: *Settings › You › Noctorium account*.
- **Signing in with Telegram, in Telegram.** *Continue with Telegram* opens Noctorium's bot in the Telegram app: press
  Start, then Confirm, and you are signed in. No phone number is typed into a web page.
- **A new look for signing in.** The Noctorium logo, and Discord and Telegram buttons with their own marks.

## Fixed

- The terminal player's Settings search finds *Sign in with Discord, Telegram or GitHub*.
