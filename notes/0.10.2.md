Spotify's library: no more silence when Spotify says no.

## Fixed

- **Connecting Spotify could show nothing, and say nothing.** When Spotify refused to hand over the library,
  the library quietly left Spotify out as long as another service's playlists were there to show. Now the
  reason is shown above them, and on the Spotify card in Settings, which no longer says your playlists are
  in the library while Spotify is refusing them.
- **Spotify's own reason is kept.** At the moment Spotify reads nothing through a developer app unless the
  account that owns the app has Spotify Premium, and it says so in plain words — which Noctorium used to
  drop, suggesting instead that your account was missing from a list. It now tells you exactly what Spotify
  said.
- **Spotify connected while the library was loading** was left out of it until the listing went stale, five
  minutes later. Connecting an account now always reloads the library.

## Worth knowing

- Until the account that owns Noctorium's Spotify app has Spotify Premium, connecting Spotify signs you in and
  then says why the library cannot be read, instead of showing an empty one.
- Noctorium is otherwise the same as in 0.10.1 on every system; only the number has moved.
