The remote control wears Noctorium's logo and can be made your own, and every Noctorium is version 0.29.1.

## New

**Customize the remote control.** The ⚙ at the top of the remote's page (<http://localhost:6932>, once *Settings ›
This device › Remote control* is on) opens its own settings. They are kept in that browser, so a phone on a stand and
your computer can each look the way you want.

- **Colours.** Noctorium's own theme, changing when you change it in the app, or Dark, Black or Light.
- **Accent.** Noctorium's, one of seven colours, any colour you pick, or the colour of the cover playing, which
  changes with every song.
- **Only the player.** Leave out the tabs for a small window or a phone beside your speakers, with the cover large.
- **What shows.** Hide shuffle and repeat, like, the volume, the speed and sleep row, the service's name, or any tab,
  and choose the tab the page opens on.
- **Lyrics.** Four sizes, left or centred, with or without the glow on the line being sung, and following the song
  or left where you scroll.
- **Shape and size.** Round, soft or square corners, four text sizes, a small, medium or large cover, compact lists,
  and how strongly the cover glows behind the player.
- **A name of its own.** “Living room” on the page and in the browser tab, say.
- **Keyboard.** Switch the shortcuts off, or choose how far ← → seek and ↑ ↓ change the volume. **M** mutes and **L**
  likes the song.
- **Back to how it was** undoes all of it.

**Noctorium's logo** is on the remote's page and its browser tab, in place of the plain “N”.

## Worth knowing

- The remote control is on the Windows, Mac and Linux desktop app, not yet on the phone or in Noctorium Rust.
- Every Noctorium — the desktop, the phone, the CLI, Noctorium Stats on both, Noctorium Rust, Noctorium Song Find on
  both, the web player, the website, the account service and the installers — is numbered 0.29.1.
