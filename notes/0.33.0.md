Artists' pages with everything on them, Playlists in Search, and your liked songs first. Every Noctorium is version 0.33.0.

## New

**An artist's page shows all of them now**, on the computer, the phone, the TV, Noctorium Rust, the web player and in
the terminal:

- **Popular**: their most played songs first.
- **Albums, Singles & EPs and Playlists**, row by row, as their page on the service has them. An album opens like any
  playlist, and Back goes back to the artist.
- On YouTube Music, their banner across the top, their bio and how many follow them (7.22M for Daft Punk).
- Then the rest of their songs.

**Playlists in Search**, beside Music, Radio and Artists: playlists by name or mood — "lofi", "gym", "90s hits" — from
YouTube Music's and SoundCloud's own playlist search. On the computer they fill the page as cards, and they arrive
service by service rather than all waiting for the slowest.

**Your liked songs head the library**: Liked Music, Liked tracks, Liked Songs — each service's — as a wide card of
its own at the top. On the TV they are a row of their own, and in the car they come first.

**On the phone, pull down at the top of Home to search**, as Ctrl+F does on the computer: the first songs (or
stations, artists or playlists) are right under the bar, and "All results" goes to Search.

**Home layout** in Settings. There is one layout so far, Classic; more are on their way.

## Fixed

- **SoundCloud songs that said "not available" on the phone play now.** Some tracks only come as a kind of stream the
  phone's reader could not open; Noctorium now asks SoundCloud itself for them, as the computer already managed.
- The web player at noctorium-music.vercel.app has all of this too.
