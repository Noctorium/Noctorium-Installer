## New

**Toolbar 2**, a player bar of its own beside the Toolbar: the same flat buttons, without the add button and with
play and pause as one button again, and more of them along the row. Pick it under **Settings → Player bar layout**,
or search Settings for "toolbar 2".

- **On the computer:** previous, play and pause, next; the song, the slider and the time; then like, lyrics and
  the queue, repeat and shuffle, the sleep timer, Connect, the speed and the volume.
- **On the phone:** the song over a row of like, previous, play and pause, next, the queue, shuffle and repeat, over
  the slider and the time.
- **In the browser and in the terminal:** the computer's row, with what each can do — the web player you open
  online has no Connect or speed to offer, so it leaves those out.

Any of its buttons can be put away under **Buttons on the player bar**, and Stop is there when you ask for it, as on
the other bars. In a narrower window, lyrics and the queue give way first, then the toggles, the sleep timer and the
speed — unless a timer is counting down or the speed is not 1× — while previous, play and pause, next, the slider
and the volume always stay. It is drawn in every theme, Windows 98, Noctorium 98 and XP among them.
