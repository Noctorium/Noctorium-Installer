The music no longer stops when you switch apps on phones that restrict Noctorium in the background — OnePlus's above
all — and Noctorium now says when a phone does that, with the switch a tap away.

## Fixed

- **Music stopping when you switch apps, until you went back to Noctorium.** On a phone where Noctorium's "Allow
  background activity" is off — Android calls it Restricted — Android takes Noctorium's music out of the foreground
  the moment you leave it, and phones such as OnePlus's then freeze it. Noctorium now notices:
  - **A warning above the player bar** while the phone restricts it: "Your phone restricts Noctorium in the background,
    so the music stops when you switch apps", with **Allow**, which opens Noctorium's own battery page, where
    Unrestricted — on a OnePlus, Allow background activity — is chosen. It goes away by itself once that is done.
  - **Once, when the music starts** while restricted, the same in a few words.
  - **When you come back** after the music was stopped in the background, what happened and why, with the same button.
- **The music takes its place back.** Whenever the music is playing and Android has taken it out of the foreground,
  Noctorium takes it back as soon as it is allowed — on the next song, every ten seconds while it plays, and the moment
  you open it — rather than going on playing as an ordinary background app that a phone may freeze. This works with
  Keep Noctorium alive on or off.
- **Keep Noctorium alive's checklist** has "Not restricted in the background" as a line of its own, and on OnePlus,
  Oppo and realme phones "Allow background activity (App info › Battery usage)". The section also counts the times the
  music was stopped in the background.

## Worth knowing

- Being restricted in the background is the phone's setting, and no app can change it for itself: the button takes you
  to it, and the choice is yours.
