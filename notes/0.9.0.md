Making Noctorium yours: how it sounds, what colour and typeface it wears, how the lyrics look, and what it
shows you.

## New

**An equaliser**, on the computer and on the phone, under Settings → Sound. Ten bands from the deepest bass
to the air above the cymbals, each moving up to twelve decibels either way, and a preamp. The presets are a
gentle place to start — Bass boost, Less bass, Treble boost, Vocals, Rock, Pop, Electronic, Hip-hop, Jazz,
Classical, Acoustic, Quiet listening and Spoken word — and moving any band makes the curve your own, starting
from the preset you were on.

- A curve that lifts anything is turned down by half its highest lift first, so a boost does not clip.
- Switching the equaliser off keeps your curve for when you switch it back on, and choosing a preset keeps it
  under Your own.
- On the phone it works through the phone's own equaliser, whose bands differ from phone to phone; each of
  them follows your curve between ours.
- In the Noctorium CLI, Settings has an Equaliser row that steps through Off and the presets.

**A colour of your own.** Next to the accents there is Your own: a colour square, a hue strip and a box for a
hex value. Everything recolours as you drag, and on the computer it tells you when the colour would be hard
to see on the theme you are using.

**A typeface**: the system's own, a serif like the notes in a record sleeve, or a monospace like a terminal.
Every word changes, the player and the menus included.

**How the lyrics look**: their size, from Small to Huge; lined up as each layout has them, to the left, or
centred; and whether the lines not being sung are dimmed. Off sets every line at full strength, with the one
being sung in the accent. On the computer it is in Customization → Lyrics, on the phone in Settings → Lyrics.

**Put away what you do not use.**

- The player's buttons: shuffle, repeat, like, lyrics, the queue, the sleep timer, volume and Connect can each
  be hidden. Play, pause and the two skips always stay, and a hidden sleep timer or Connect still appears
  while it is in use.
- Home's parts: the greeting, what you pinned, what you played lately, and the rows from YouTube Music or from
  SoundCloud. Put everything away and Home says so, with a way back.
- The sidebar on the computer and the tabs on the phone. Home and Settings always stay, and on the phone the
  queue does too. On the computer a page left out of the sidebar is still a number key away.

**Announcing tracks**, on the computer: a small note with the title and the artist when a new song starts.
It is off unless you switch it on under Settings → Startup and tray, and it stays quiet while you are looking
at Noctorium and for the same song twice in a row.

## Fixed

- **On the phone, under liquid glass, a text field low on a page was typed into behind the player and the
  tabs**, which ride up on the keyboard. While the keyboard is up the page now ends where they begin, so the
  field you are typing in sits just above them.

## Worth knowing

- Everything here starts the way Noctorium has always looked and sounded: the equaliser is off, the accent is
  the theme's own, and nothing is hidden until you hide it.
- While tracks are announced the tray icon stays, even with the window open, because Windows sends the note
  from the icon.
