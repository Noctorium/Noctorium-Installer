Radio from every country in Noctorium, Noctorium Song Find telling you when it has found the song, and every
Noctorium is version 0.28.0.

## New

**Radio.** Noctorium plays live radio now, on the desktop, on the phone and in Noctorium Rust: tens of thousands of
stations from more than two hundred countries, from [Radio Browser](https://www.radio-browser.info), an open list of
the world's stations that needs no account.

- **Music or Radio, beside the search's heading.** A small *Music ▾* next to *Search* switches what the search
  finds. Music is what it always is when Noctorium starts.
- **A country, or everywhere.** With Radio chosen, the service buttons make way for a country picker — type a name
  to find one in the list — and the stations are the most listened-to there. Type a station, a city or a kind of
  music, “jazz”, “BBC”, “Tokyo”, to look for something in particular.
- **For the keyboard.** In the quick search bar, Ctrl+F (⌘F on a Mac) again switches between Music and Radio. With
  Radio on and nothing typed, the bar already lists the most listened-to stations: Ctrl+F, Ctrl+F, Enter, and the
  radio is on.
- **Live, and left alone.** The player says *LIVE* where a song's length would be. Stations are not scrobbled to
  Last.fm or the others, and nothing autoplays after one.

**Noctorium Song Find tells you when it has found the song.** When you are in another window or app while it
listens, a notification says which song it was, with its cover, or that no song was found — click or tap it to go
back. On Android the listening notification also counts down the seconds left. Settings can turn it off.

## Fixed

- **Noctorium Song Find on Android kept listening after you switched apps** — before, the microphone went silent
  in the background and the search ended with nothing heard. It also asks once whether it may show notifications.
- **Noctorium Song Find no longer shows the last song beside a new problem,** as if the failed search had found it.
- **On the desktop, Song Find's History** no longer shows a different song in the one you had open after a new
  song is found; **Space** no longer starts a search and stops it again at once; and **an audio file dropped while
  it is listening** says to drop it again when the search is done, instead of doing nothing.

## Worth knowing

- A station plays whatever its broadcaster sends, at the broadcaster's quality; Radio Browser lists stations that
  were working when it last looked, but one can go quiet at any time.
- Every Noctorium — the desktop, the phone, the CLI, Noctorium Stats on both, Noctorium Rust, Noctorium Song Find on
  both, the web player, the website, the account service and the installers — is numbered 0.28.0.
