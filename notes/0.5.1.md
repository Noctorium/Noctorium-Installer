Liquid glass, done properly this time.

The version in 0.5.0 was translucent panels over a tinted page — frosted glass with a new name. It had
none of the three things that make the real effect, and it put the glass in the wrong place. This
replaces it completely.

## What changed

**The glass is on the controls, and the music scrolls under it.** The player and the tabs float as
panes, and the lists run the full height of the screen beneath them. That is the only arrangement in
which there is anything to see through glass: covers pass under the tab bar as you scroll, and you
watch them go. Cards and lists are solid again; making content see-through only made it harder to
read.

**It bends what's behind it.** Each pane is a lens. The middle is clear, and at the rounded edge the
content just beyond the pane is pulled in and wrapped round the rim, the way a thick slab of glass
bends it — with a faint split of colour at the edge, as real glass has. This is the part that makes it
look like glass rather than like a blur.

**It frosts what is actually behind it**, lightly: a cover under the tab bar is still recognisably
that cover, and a line of text behind the player is just a line rather than words competing with the
player's own.

**Its edge catches the light** — bright along the top, fainter along the bottom.

The same lens runs on the phone and on the desktop.

## Cost

About the same as Solid. With music playing and nothing moving, the phone draws exactly as many frames
as it does under Solid and uses a few percent more of one core; the desktop uses the same. An earlier
build of this doubled both, and that was fixed before release.

## Worth knowing

- The bending needs **Android 13 or newer**. On Android 12 the panes are frosted without the lens, and
  on older phones they are tinted with the rim alone. All of them still float.
- With the player bar set to the **top** on the desktop, pages start below the player rather than
  scrolling under it, so no page title ever ends up hidden behind it.
- Solid is still the default. Liquid glass is under Settings → Customization → Surfaces.

Nothing else has changed since 0.5.0.
