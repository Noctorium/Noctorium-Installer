Apple Music, Deezer and Tidal, Libre.fm for scrobbling, and a Windows install and updates several times faster.

## New

**Apple Music, Deezer and Tidal**, on the computer, the phone, in the terminal and in the browser `noctorium web`
serves. Search them, open their albums, artists and playlists, paste their links, and see your library from them on
Home and in the library — each with its own badge, and `!am`, `!dz` and `!td` in search. None of them lets another
app play its music, so, as with Spotify, each song is matched to the same recording on YouTube Music when it plays.

- **Deezer** searches straight away. Give your Deezer profile under Settings → Deezer and its public playlists, loved
  tracks and albums are in your library.
- **Apple Music** searches straight away. Sign in on Apple's own page under Settings → Apple Music for your library;
  if the sign-in window will not finish, its token can be pasted instead.
- **Tidal** needs a free developer app of your own, made at developer.tidal.com: Settings → Tidal walks through it,
  with the address to give the app, then signs you in on Tidal's own page.

**A beta warning** at the top of the settings of every service still in beta — Spotify, Bandcamp, VK, Apple Music,
Deezer and Tidal: they may not work as they should, and there is a small chance one gets your account blocked. Use
them at your own risk. YouTube Music, YouTube and SoundCloud carry none.

**Libre.fm**, the free scrobbling service, beside Last.fm and ListenBrainz: sign in on its page as you do Last.fm's,
and every listen goes to it too.

**The sign-in link to copy.** While Last.fm or Libre.fm waits for you to allow Noctorium, its page is shown with
**Copy link** and **Open again**, so it can be opened in any browser; allowing it there connects Noctorium by itself.

## Faster

- **The Windows installer** downloads over sixteen connections at once instead of four, and asks for the release and
  its checksums together: Noctorium down and checked in about 12 seconds where it took about 45 on the same line.
- **Updates from inside Noctorium** come down the same way: the Windows package in about 12 seconds instead of over
  two minutes, the phone's in about 2 instead of 8, and one stopped part of the way carries on from where it got to.
- **A tenth smaller**: only the icons Noctorium draws are packed, over 30 MB less on Windows, Linux and the Mac. And an
  upgrade on Windows no longer stops to make a System Restore point while it takes the old version away.

## Fixed

- **On Linux, Last.fm's sign-in opened no browser.** Links now go to the desktop's own opener, `xdg-open` first, the
  way every Linux program opens them — on the computer and in the terminal.
- **Settings, sign-ins and playlists could go missing from disk on Windows** if something else was reading the file
  at the moment Noctorium saved it, such as the terminal reading the desktop's settings. They are now never missing.
- Pressing Finish on a Last.fm sign-in that was never begun no longer leaves it waiting for nothing.

## Worth knowing

- Devices on 0.17 and earlier cannot be handed a queue holding Apple Music, Deezer or Tidal songs over Noctorium
  Connect. Update every device.
- Apple Music is reached the way Apple's own web player reaches it, which its terms do not allow, as with VK.
