A fix for links in search. Every Noctorium is version 0.34.1.

## Fixed

- **A music link in the search bar takes you to the song.** Paste a YouTube Music, YouTube, SoundCloud, Spotify,
  Bandcamp or other music link into Search and it plays at once; type one out and press Enter, and it plays too. It
  works in the search on Home that a pull down shows on the phone, on the Search page, in the computer's Ctrl+F bar,
  in the terminal and in Noctorium Rust. "Play links pasted into Search" is now on unless you switch it off, and a
  link pasted over another one plays the new one.
- **No more "Nothing found" under a link.** A link in the box is no longer searched for as words: the search says
  it is a music link, and Enter plays it.
