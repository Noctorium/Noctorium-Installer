Artists' pages, Artists in Search, SoundCloud that plays, and a new Noctorium whenever yt-dlp has one. Every Noctorium
is version 0.32.0.

## New

**Press an artist's name to open their page.** Under the song in the player bar, on Now playing, and in a song's menu
("Go to …"): the artist opens on the service the song came from — the YouTube Music artist for a YouTube Music song, the
SoundCloud profile for a SoundCloud one, the Spotify, Apple Music, Deezer, Tidal, Bandcamp or VK artist for theirs —
with their songs, to play or shuffle like a playlist.

- On the TV, the artist under Now playing opens their songs.
- In the car, the Queue starts with "More by" the artist playing.
- In the terminal, `g` opens the artist of the song selected, or of the one playing.

**Artists in Search.** Beside Music and Radio in Search's menu: type a name and the artists come back from every
service you search, each opening their page. `Ctrl+F` goes round all three. The TV lists artists above the songs it
finds, and so does the car's search.

**Play links pasted into Search.** A YouTube, YouTube Music, SoundCloud, Spotify or other music link pasted into the
search box can play at once, without the Link page. It is off to begin with: switch it on under Settings › Search.

**SoundCloud plays more.** When SoundCloud won't play a track — it is locked to SoundCloud's own app, kept for Go+
subscribers, not offered in your country, or has been taken down — Noctorium now plays the same song from YouTube
Music instead and says why, rather than stopping with a line of the extractor's own words. The reasons are said
plainly where no match is found.

**A new Noctorium whenever yt-dlp has one.** yt-dlp is what reads YouTube and SoundCloud on the computer, and an old one
stops playing things. From now on, the day yt-dlp releases, a Noctorium with the next patch number (0.32.1, 0.32.2…)
comes out with it by itself, so your updater, the installers and Obtainium offer the new yt-dlp at once.

## Worth knowing

- The new yt-dlp releases change nothing else: each is the release before it, with the new yt-dlp inside.
- Every Noctorium — the desktop, the phone with its car and TV, the CLI, Noctorium Stats on both, Noctorium Rust,
  Noctorium Song Find on both, the web player, the website, the account service and the installers — is numbered
  0.32.0.
