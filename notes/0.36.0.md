More ways into your Noctorium account, and what you play on your Discord and Telegram. Every Noctorium is version 0.36.0.

## New

- **Sign in with Discord, Telegram or GitHub.** Every Noctorium with an account — desktop, phone, TV, the terminal app,
  Noctorium Rust and both Noctorium Stats — has a new button for it. It shows a code and opens
  service.noctorium.xyz in the browser; sign in there any way you like, press Allow, and the app signs itself in. On a
  TV or in a terminal, open the page on your phone and type the code. Email and password work as before.
- **Several ways into one account.** On service.noctorium.xyz, under *Ways to sign in*, link Discord, GitHub and
  Telegram to your account, or add a password to an account you made with one of them.
- **What you play, on Discord and Telegram.** Link them on your account's page and the song you are playing shows as
  your Discord status and in your Telegram bio, from any Noctorium you are signed in to.
- **noctorium.xyz.** The website, the account service and the web player have their own addresses now:
  noctorium.xyz, service.noctorium.xyz and music.noctorium.xyz.
