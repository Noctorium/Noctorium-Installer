Noctorium Stats: your listening, in an app of its own, on Windows, macOS, Linux and Android.

## New

**Noctorium Stats.** Sign in with your Noctorium account — the one Noctorium counts your plays in — and see
what you have listened to. Pick the last 7 days, 30 days, 90 days, the year or all of it, and it shows:

- the songs you streamed, how many different ones, the artists and the hours;
- your listening day by day, or month by month over a longer span, and the busiest day;
- how the services share it, YouTube Music, SoundCloud, Spotify, Bandcamp and VK each in their own colour;
- your top songs and top artists, with their plays and minutes;
- the hours of the day and the days of the week you listen most, on your own clock;
- what you played last, and how many days in a row you have listened, with your longest run.

On the computer it is a window of its own, written in Rust; on the phone, an app written in Dart with
Flutter. Your sign-in is kept in the system's keyring on the computer and in the phone's keystore. When there
is no connection it says so, and the computer shows the figures it last had. Nothing is counted until you
sign in to the same account in Noctorium and play something.

**In every installer.** The PC installer has a third box for it, and `noctorium-installer-cli` a fourth
choice (`--product stats`, or `all` for everything). The one-line installers ask about it, and the phone
installer has a button for it.

- On Windows it is installed for you alone, with no permission asked: in your own Programs folder, in the
  Start menu, and in Apps & features to remove.
- On Linux it goes in `~/.local`, with a menu entry; on a Mac, Noctorium Stats goes in Applications.

**The statistics site,** [noctorium-service.vercel.app](https://noctorium-service.vercel.app), reads the same
account in a browser.

## Changed

- The account service now gives its statistics for a range of days, on the listener's own clock, with top
  artists, the charts and the streak beside the totals. Asked as Noctorium always has, it answers as before.
- Noctorium's updater, the installers and the website never take one of Noctorium Stats' files for
  Noctorium's own, however a release happens to list them.

## Worth knowing

- A release now has 27 files. Noctorium Stats' are `noctorium-stats-0.13.0-windows-x64.zip`,
  `-linux-x64.tar.gz`, `-macos-arm64.zip`, `-macos-x64.zip` and `Noctorium-Stats-0.13.0.apk`, which is
  signed with the same key as Noctorium.
- On a Mac, Noctorium Stats is not signed with an Apple developer identity either: downloaded in a browser,
  the first time choose Open Anyway in System Settings, Privacy & Security. From the one line, it is not
  asked about.
