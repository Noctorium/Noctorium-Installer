Two new Noctoriums — Noctorium Lite for any Android phone, and Noctorium for iPhone and iPad — and a tidier website. Every Noctorium is version 0.35.0.

## New

- **Noctorium Lite**, `Noctorium-Lite-0.35.0.apk`: Noctorium for Android in about 125 KB, against the full app's twenty
  megabytes, for any phone from Android 5. Home, search on YouTube Music and SoundCloud, your liked songs and history,
  playlists, a queue with Play next, autoplay, links, and the lock screen and notification — with nothing but what
  Android itself has inside.
- **Noctorium for iPhone and iPad**, `Noctorium-0.35.0-ios.ipa`: Home, search, your liked songs and playlists of your
  own, synced lyrics that follow the song, the queue, autoplay, a sleep timer, and the lock screen and Control Center.
  Not on the App Store: install it with AltStore or SideStore.
- **The website has one Download button** and a download page with every way to get Noctorium, sorted by device.
