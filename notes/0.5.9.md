Long playlists and long lists of likes, read to the end.

## Fixed

**Long playlists open whole.** A playlist opened with only its first two hundred songs, and the rest could
not be played, shuffled or downloaded. Now it opens whole, up to the five thousand YouTube lets a playlist
hold. On the phone, where a playlist could also stop at its first hundred, every page is read too.

**Every SoundCloud like counts.** SoundCloud sends likes two hundred at a time, and only the first two
hundred were read: older likes showed an empty heart, and your liked tracks stopped there. Every page is
read now.

**Public SoundCloud playlists open whole on the phone.** A public SoundCloud playlist could open with only
its first few tracks; one of four hundred showed four. It is now read straight from SoundCloud, as private
ones already were, and opens in a few seconds.

## Worth knowing

- A very long playlist takes a moment longer to open, since every page of it is asked for. Covers are
  only looked up for the songs that are missing one.
