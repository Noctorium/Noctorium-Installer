Stream widgets for OBS and Streamlabs on the computer, and four Glow themes on every Noctorium: near-black pages lit
by a soft light, Afterglow's in the colour of the cover playing.

## New

**Stream widgets.** Show what Noctorium is playing over your stream. Switch them on under Settings → Stream widgets,
copy a look's address, and add it in OBS as a Browser source (Sources, +, Browser), with the width and height shown
beside it. Streamlabs takes the same address as a Browser Source. Seven looks:

- **Glass** — a frosted dark card with the cover, the title and the artist, the service's logo and a thin progress bar.
- **Minimal** — only words: a small "Now playing", the title and the artist, readable over any game.
- **Vinyl** — a record that turns while the music plays, with the cover as its label and the song beside it.
- **Neon** — a dark see-through strip with a glowing outline, the song written in light in your accent colour.
- **Ticker** — a slim bar for the edge of the screen, scrolling gently when the title is too long.
- **Afterglow** — a large cover over a soft glow in its own colours, with the title and the artist beneath. Tall, for a
  corner of the stream.
- **Windows 98** — a tiny Now Playing window from 1998, with a navy title bar and a progress bar of blocks.

Each look has its own address, so a stream can carry several at once. Choose whether they show the cover, how far into
the song it is and the service it comes from, and whether they fade away a few seconds after the music is paused. A
change reaches a widget already on your stream within a second. They are off until you switch them on, and only this
computer can open them unless you allow other devices on your network, for a second computer that does the streaming.
Tick "Refresh browser when scene becomes active" in OBS, so a widget added before Noctorium was open finds it.

**Four Glow themes**, on the computer, the phone, in the terminal and in the browser:

- **Afterglow** — a warm black page lit by a soft glow in the colour of the cover playing, red when there is none. With
  the Sing along layout, Now playing is the lyrics floating on the light of the record.
- **Cinder** — Afterglow's page with its red held still, for the look without it changing with every song.
- **Velvet** — flat black with a faint plum light across the top, and white as the only accent.
- **Smoke** — Velvet's blacks with a grey light, and no colour at all.

On a Glow theme the colour wash behind Now playing gives way to the theme's own light; a blurred cover, chosen on
purpose, is still drawn. Where white is the accent, a switched-on button such as shuffle or repeat is marked with a dot
or a disc, since its colour alone would not say so. In a terminal the themes are their colours, without the light.

## Better

- "Match the artwork" really follows the cover on the phone, where it used to keep the theme's own accent, and in the
  page `noctorium web` serves.
- Writing on a bright accent is worked out the same way everywhere, so the browser and the terminal now put the same
  dark label as the computer on accents such as Gruvbox's or Catppuccin's.

## Worth knowing

- Stream widgets need OBS 28 or later, or a Streamlabs of the same age.
- With other devices allowed, Windows will ask once whether Noctorium may be reached on your network.
