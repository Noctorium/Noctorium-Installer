Your YouTube Music playlists, edited from the phone too.

## New

**Public, unlisted or private, from the phone.** Open one of your own YouTube Music playlists and three chips say
who can see it — **Public**, **Unlisted** or **Private** — with what that means under them; one tap changes it.
**Edit** changes the name, the description and who can see it in one save, as on the computer, and **Delete** sits
beside it. Playlists you only saved from someone else show none of these, and the library now says when a playlist
is unlisted as well as when it is private. SoundCloud playlists are as they were.

- If YouTube turns a change down, the message says so in red rather than in the colour of one that went through.
