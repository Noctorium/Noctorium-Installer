YouTube Music sign-in and account controls, done the way SimpMusic does them.

## If YouTube Music now says your sign-in has expired

It had. A YouTube sign-in that lapses is not refused. YouTube quietly starts answering as though
nobody were signed in, so Noctorium went on saying "signed in" while likes and playlists came back
empty. It now checks, and tells you. Sign in again once. On the desktop, the quickest way is from
your phone (below).

## New

**Sign in on the desktop with your phone.** Settings → YouTube Music → **Sign in with your phone**
shows a QR code. In Noctorium on the phone, Settings → YouTube Music → **Send to computer**, and
point the camera at it. The phone sends over the sign-in it already has, encrypted with a key that
exists only in that code, so nobody else on your Wi-Fi can read it. The code works once, for five
minutes. The camera is only asked for when you scan.

**Stays signed in.** Noctorium checks shortly after it opens and every few hours. When YouTube's part
of the sign-in has only gone stale, it renews it the way a browser does, using your Google sign-in. If
Google itself has signed you out, it says so and asks you to sign in again. Sign-ins now keep what
they need for this, so they last better from here on.

**Pick the right account and channel.** Every Google account signed in, and every channel each one
owns, with names and pictures. Noctorium used to act as the first Google account whatever you had
chosen, which went wrong for a brand channel of any other account.

**Your plays go into your YouTube Music history**, after a few seconds of listening, so YouTube Music's
own recommendations learn from what you play here. On by default, with a switch in Settings →
YouTube Music.

**Follow artists.** A Follow button under the artist on the now playing screen, for YouTube songs.

**Reorder your YouTube playlists.** Up and down arrows beside each song in playlists that are yours.

**Paste cookies.** For advanced use: sign in from a cookies.txt, a cookie-editor export or a Cookie
header, when the sign-in window will not do.

## Fixed

- The sign-in window no longer keeps its own copy of your YouTube session once Noctorium has saved one.
- Requests now identify themselves as the same browser you signed in with. A phone used to sign in as
  Chrome on Android and then ask as Chrome on Windows, which looks to Google like a second device.

## Worth knowing

- Sign-ins made before this version can't be renewed, because they were saved without the part that
  renewal needs. If one lapses, sign in again once; later sign-ins can be renewed.
- History, following and reordering change your YouTube Music account, just as doing them there would.
