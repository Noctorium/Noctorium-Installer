Bandcamp, VK Music and Spotify played on Spotify, autoplay from the song's own service, and a queue you keep.

## New

**Bandcamp.** Search it, open its albums and artists, play its songs, and get rows of its best-sellers and new
releases on Home, with genres you pick. There is nothing to sign in to: give your Bandcamp name, the end of
bandcamp.com/<name>, and your collection and wishlist are in the library. Paste a Bandcamp link to play it.
Bandcamp songs are bought, not downloaded: their menus open the Bandcamp page instead.

**Spotify, two ways.**

- *Spotify*, for any account: your playlists and Liked Songs in the library, hearts that save to and take out
  of Liked Songs, Spotify in search with its albums and artists, and two rows on Home: your top songs and what
  you played lately. Songs are matched on YouTube Music and played from there, as before.
- *Spotify Premium*: all of that, and Spotify songs play on Spotify itself, in your own Spotify app, on this
  computer, your phone or a speaker. Pick where in Settings. Noctorium tells Spotify what to play and follows
  along; it never decodes Spotify's audio. Keep Spotify open somewhere.

**VK Music.** Sign in on VK's own page, inside Noctorium (or paste the session's two cookies in the terminal):
My music and your VK playlists in the library, VK in search, VK's suggestions and what is popular there on
Home, and a heart that adds a song to My music. Read the notice before signing in: VK does not offer its music
to other apps, its terms do not allow this, it may freeze an account it thinks is automated, many songs do not
play outside Russia, and VK songs cannot be downloaded.

**Up next.** When the queue is about to run out, what comes after it is lined up underneath, from the service of
the song that ends it:

- after a YouTube Music song, YouTube Music's radio;
- after a SoundCloud song, SoundCloud's related tracks;
- after a Bandcamp song, more from the artist;
- after a VK song, VK's suggestions;
- after a Spotify song played on Spotify, Spotify's own autoplay, which Noctorium follows; matched, more from
  the same artists on Spotify, since Spotify no longer gives apps its recommendations.

Next goes straight into them. Play one, keep one in the queue or drop it. Prefer one radio for everything?
*Autoplay from: YouTube Music radio* does that, and autoplay can be switched off.

**The queue is kept.** Close Noctorium and the queue is there next time, with the song you were in picked up
where you left it. Shuffle or clear what is still to come, or save the whole queue as a playlist.

## More ways to make it yours

- **Playback speed**, from half to double, keeping the pitch.
- **Which services answer Hybrid search**: leave out any you do not use.
- **The search mode you chose last** is the one search opens in.
- **A sleep timer that fades out** over its last 15, 30 or 60 seconds, and puts the volume back afterwards.
- On the phone, a **data saver** that plays smaller audio on mobile data, or always.
- In the terminal, **keys you choose**: rebind any action in Settings.
- **Paste a Spotify or VK link** -- a song, an album, a playlist or an artist -- and it opens, as YouTube, SoundCloud and Bandcamp links do.

## Fixed

- Only YouTube's own songs are sent to a YouTube Music playlist. Before, a song from anywhere else could be
  sent there and only failed.
- Two settings changed a moment apart could fail to save, or the first could undo the second at the next
  launch.
- Home's service chips scroll sideways on a narrow phone instead of crushing the last one, and the desktop's
  greeting stays whole in a narrow window.
- Signing in to YouTube on the phone no longer signs SoundCloud out with it: each sign-in clears only its own
  service's cookies.
- Next is greyed where it leads nowhere, and lit again as soon as autoplay has something lined up.

## Worth knowing

- Spotify through Noctorium's own Spotify app works once the account that owns that app has Spotify Premium,
  which is Spotify's rule for apps in development mode. Spotify also asks everyone to sign in again every six
  months now.
- Autoplay is on for everyone from this version; switch it off under Settings if you would rather the queue
  stopped.
- VK Music and playing Spotify songs on Spotify are brand new. If something there does not work for you, say so
  on GitHub.
