Your full stats in every Noctorium, the phone as a dedicated music player, and Noctorium Account. Every Noctorium is version 0.43.0.

## New

- **Full stats in every app.** Tap your picture and choose **Stats**. Your Noctorium account's listening from every device
  shows over 7 days, 30 days, 90 days, a year or all time:
  - listens, time listened, songs and artists;
  - your current and longest streak of days in a row;
  - a day-by-day chart (month by month over the longest spans);
  - the hours of the day and days of the week you listen most;
  - top songs and artists, the split by service, and what you played last.

  It's on the phone, the desktop, the web player and `noctorium web`, Noctorium Rust, the iPhone and Noctorium Lite. On
  the phone, signed out, Stats still counts what this phone played.
- **stats.noctorium.xyz** shows all of it too, with the same spans and charts.
- **Player mode** (phone). Turns a phone into a dedicated music player: Noctorium becomes its home screen. It shows the
  clock and battery, the song playing with big buttons, Search, Library, Queue and Noctorium, and Wi-Fi and Bluetooth.
  - **Use a spare phone, not the one you use every day.** Your other apps leave the home screen.
  - **Turn it on** in *Settings → On this phone → Player mode*.
  - **Leave** by holding the clock.
- **Noctorium Account**, for Windows, macOS, Linux and Android. It does what account.noctorium.xyz does, without a
  browser:
  - your picture, name, email and password;
  - two-step verification: an authenticator app with its QR code and backup codes, or codes on Telegram;
  - the Last.fm and ListenBrainz your apps scrobble to;
  - deleting the account.

  The files are `noctorium-account-*` and `Noctorium-Account-*.apk`.
