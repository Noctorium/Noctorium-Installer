Noctorium for Android is now three apps, one for each kind of device: the phone, the TV, and the car. Every Noctorium is version 0.38.0.

## New

- **An APK for each device.**
  - `Noctorium-0.38.0.apk`: phones, with **Android Auto** still inside, since Android Auto always runs from the phone.
  - `Noctorium-TV-0.38.0.apk`: **Android TV and Google TV**, with the TV's own screens.
  - `Noctorium-Car-0.38.0.apk`: **cars with Google built in** (Android Automotive), installed on the car itself. Most
    cars only install apps from their own Play Store; it installs on cars and head units that allow apps from
    elsewhere.
- **TVs move over by themselves.** A TV updating from 0.37 or older gets the phone's app first, which still opens from
  the TV's home screen; its updater then offers **Noctorium for TV**, and installing it keeps everything: sign-ins,
  library, downloads and settings.
- The Noctorium Installer app installs any of the three, and the website and Obtainium have each one.
